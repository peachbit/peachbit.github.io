MonitorSwitcher
Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-01

MonitorSwitcher ("the app"), made by Peachbit, switches the input, brightness and volume of the monitors connected to your PC over DDC/CI, a communication channel on the video cable.

  1. No data is collected. The app does not collect any information — personal or not — such as names, email addresses, location, usage statistics or crash reports.
  2. The app itself makes no network connections. It never connects to the internet or to any server, and contains no advertising, analytics or third-party SDKs. The trial period, purchase status, purchases, and checking for and installing new versions are handled by the Microsoft Store service built into Windows, under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. Redeeming a promo code opens Microsoft's code redemption page in your browser.
  3. Stored only on your PC. Settings (language, theme, input names, hotkeys, scenes, etc.) and a diagnostic log are stored only in the app's own folder on this PC. They are never transmitted, and they are removed when the app is uninstalled.
  4. Communication with monitors. The app only reads and changes input, brightness and volume, and reads each monitor's model name and capabilities.
  5. Windows display settings. The app reads each display's resolution, refresh rate, orientation and main display setting through Windows to show them, and changes them only when you ask it to. So that a change can be undone, it keeps the previous settings on your PC for a short time. They are never sent anywhere.
  6. Contact. For questions about this policy, email peachbitsoft@outlook.com (Peachbit).
